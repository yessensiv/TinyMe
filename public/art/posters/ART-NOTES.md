# Постеры MyRoom 0.24

## Вариант v3 — для уютного интерьера

Текущие шесть `*-v3.webp` получены редактированием соответствующих `*-v2.webp` через встроенный image_gen. Палитра и фактура приведены к рисованной комнате; композиции и узнаваемые детали сохранены.

Общий точный промпт редактирования для каждого изображения:

Edit this exact poster into a refined stylized illustration that belongs in a cozy low-poly isometric room with dusty mauve walls, muted blue shadows, warm cream lamps, sage furniture and soft peach accents. Preserve the same recognizable game subject, character identity, pose, composition, all key equipment, portrait 3:4 proportions and exact title text. Change only visual treatment: gently faceted broad painted forms, simplified fine texture, matte gouache / elegant screen print finish, softer contrast, dusty restrained colors, lavender-indigo shadow palette, warm cream highlights. Keep enough crafted detail to recognize the character and architecture; sophisticated collectible illustration, not childish cartoon, not photo, not gritty realism. Reduce excessive tiny noise and harsh black areas. Full bleed flat poster, no physical frame or room mockup. Integrate the title into the softly colored artwork.

Full bleed flat artwork viewed straight on, NO frame, no room mockup. Bottom 15 percent designed as restrained dark title area, exact legible title "[Title]" in elegant game-appropriate typography. No other text, no watermark, no publisher logos. Keep focal subject fully inside canvas.
